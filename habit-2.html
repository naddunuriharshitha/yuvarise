HABIT 2

Begin With the End in Mind

Have a clear idea of where you want to go before you begin. A clear direction can help you make better choices and stay focused on your goals.

Learn Habit 2 All 7 Habits
UNDERSTANDING THE HABIT

What Does Habit 2 Mean?

Begin With the End in Mind means thinking about your desired destination before deciding what steps to take. For students, this can mean understanding your goals, values and priorities before making important choices.

🎯

Know Your Goal

Clearly identify what you want to achieve and why it matters to you.

🧭

Choose Your Direction

Use your goals and values as a guide when making everyday decisions.

💡

Think Ahead

Consider how today's choices may affect your future plans.

🌱

Build With Purpose

Spend your time and effort on activities that support your bigger goals.

CREATE A CLEAR VISION

Where Do You Want to Go?

A simple vision can help you understand what you want to work towards.

🎓

Education

Think about the knowledge and skills you want to develop during your education.

💼

Career

Explore the type of work and career direction that interests you.

🧠

Personal Growth

Think about the qualities, habits and skills you want to develop.

🤝

Relationships

Consider how you want to communicate with and support the people around you.

FOR STUDENTS

How Can Students Use Habit 2?

📚 Study Goal

Decide what you want to understand or accomplish in a subject, then create study steps that move you towards that goal.

🎯 Career Goal

Think about a career direction you are interested in and identify the skills you need to explore.

⏰ Daily Planning

Decide what result you want from your day and then prioritize the tasks that support it.

📈 Skill Development

Choose a useful skill, define what improvement means and practice consistently.

TURN IDEAS INTO ACTION

Vision → Goal → Action

A vision becomes useful when you connect it with clear goals and practical actions.

🌟

1. Vision

Decide the direction you want your future to take.

🎯

2. Goal

Turn the direction into a specific goal that you can work towards.

📝

3. Action

Break the goal into small tasks that you can complete regularly.

🔄

4. Review

Check your progress and adjust your plan when necessary.

SELF-REFLECTION

Ask Yourself These 5 Questions

1. What Do I Want?

Identify something meaningful that you want to achieve.

2. Why Does It Matter?

Understand the reason behind your goal.

3. What Skills Do I Need?

Identify the knowledge or skills that can help you move forward.

4. What Is My Next Step?

Choose one realistic action you can take.

5. Am I Moving in the Right Direction?

Regularly review your progress and make changes when needed.

HABIT 2 CHALLENGE

Create Your Future Direction

Think about one important goal. Write down what you want to achieve, why it matters and one action you can take today.

  • 🎯 Choose one meaningful goal.
  • 📝 Write down the result you want.
  • 💡 Write why the goal matters.
  • 🚀 Choose one small action.
  • 🔄 Review your progress regularly.

KEY LESSON

Remember This

Before taking action, know what you are working towards. A clear direction can help you decide which opportunities, tasks and habits deserve your time.
