ABOUT THE BOOK

A guide for young people

The book presents personal-development principles in a teen-friendly way. Its lessons can be applied to school, friendships, goals, decision-making and everyday life.

Personal Growth

Learn how habits and everyday choices can support personal development.

Positive Mindset

Develop a constructive way of thinking and approach challenges with greater confidence.

Goals & Priorities

Understand how to set meaningful goals and focus on important priorities.

Continuous Improvement

Build the habit of learning, reflecting and improving step by step.

BOOK DETAILS

The 7 Habits of Highly Effective Teens

Author: Sean Covey

Focus: Personal development, habits, decision-making, relationships, leadership and goal setting.

The book adapts the habit-based personal-development approach for teenagers and presents ideas that can be connected with real-life situations.

THE SEVEN HABITS

Explore the seven habits

Each habit provides an idea that can be connected with everyday decisions and personal growth.

HABIT 01

Be Proactive

Focus on the choices and actions that you can control.

HABIT 02

Begin With the End in Mind

Think about what you want to achieve and create a clear direction.

HABIT 03

Put First Things First

Learn to organize priorities and focus on important tasks.

HABIT 04

Think Win-Win

Look for solutions that respect everyone's needs.

HABIT 05

Seek First to Understand

Listen carefully and try to understand others before responding.

HABIT 06

Synergize

Work together and appreciate different ideas and strengths.

HABIT 07

Sharpen the Saw

Take care of your learning, wellbeing and continuous development.
