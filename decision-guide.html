SMART CHOICES

Why Decision-Making Matters

Students make decisions every day about studies, friendships, time, goals and future plans. Learning how to think before choosing can help you make more responsible and informed choices.

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

A Simple Decision-Making Process

Use these steps whenever you face an important choice.

1️⃣

Understand the Problem

Clearly identify what decision you need to make and why it matters.

2️⃣

List Your Options

Write down the possible choices instead of deciding immediately.

3️⃣

Gather Information

Look for reliable information and understand the advantages and disadvantages of each option.

4️⃣

Think About Consequences

Consider how each choice could affect your studies, goals, relationships and future plans.

5️⃣

Choose Carefully

Compare your options and choose the one that best matches your values and goals.

6️⃣

Review Your Decision

After acting, think about what happened and what you can learn from the experience.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Ask Yourself These Questions

❓

What Do I Really Want?

Make sure you understand your actual goal before choosing an option.

🔍

What Information Do I Need?

Identify what you still need to know before making the decision.

⚖️

What Are the Consequences?

Think about the possible short-term and long-term results of your choices.

🎯

Does It Match My Goal?

Check whether your choice supports the future you are working towards.
