HABIT 3

Put First Things First

Learn to prioritize what is important, manage your time wisely and complete meaningful tasks before less important activities.

Learn Habit 3 All 7 Habits
UNDERSTANDING THE HABIT

What Does "Put First Things First" Mean?

This habit is about setting priorities. Instead of spending all your time on whatever feels easiest or most interesting, identify the important tasks and give them the attention they deserve.

🎯

Know Your Priorities

Identify the tasks and responsibilities that matter most to your goals.

⏰

Manage Your Time

Plan your time so important work gets completed without unnecessary last-minute pressure.

📋

Plan Your Day

Make a simple list of important tasks and decide when you will work on them.

🚀

Take Action

Start with the most important task instead of continually postponing it.

PRIORITIZE SMARTLY

Important vs Urgent

Not every task deserves the same amount of attention. Learning to distinguish important work from urgent distractions can improve your daily planning.

⭐

Important

Activities that support your long-term goals, learning, responsibilities and personal growth.

⚡

Urgent

Tasks that need attention quickly because of deadlines or immediate responsibilities.

🧠

Think Before Choosing

Before spending time on a task, ask whether it is important, urgent or simply a distraction.

FOR STUDENTS

How Students Can Put First Things First

📚 Study Before Distractions

Complete an important study task before spending time on entertainment or unnecessary activities.

📝 Start Assignments Early

Break assignments into smaller tasks and work on them before the deadline gets close.

🎯 Work Towards Goals

Reserve time for activities that directly support your important goals.

😴 Make Time for Rest

Good planning also includes enough rest and healthy breaks so you can continue learning well.

TIME MANAGEMENT

A Simple Daily Priority System

Use a simple system to decide what deserves your attention first.

1️⃣

Must Do

Tasks that are important and need to be completed today.

2️⃣

Should Do

Useful tasks that can be completed after your most important responsibilities.

3️⃣

Could Do

Optional activities that you can do if you have enough time.

4️⃣

Can Wait

Tasks that are not currently important and can be scheduled for later.

PRACTICE HABIT 3

5 Steps to Put First Things First

1. Write Your Tasks

Write down the important things you need to complete.

2. Identify Priorities

Decide which tasks are most important for your goals and responsibilities.

3. Schedule Them

Give important tasks a specific time in your day.

4. Reduce Distractions

Create a focused environment while working on important tasks.

5. Review Your Day

Check what you completed and decide what should be prioritized tomorrow.

HABIT 3 CHALLENGE

Choose Your Top 3 Priorities

Before starting your day, choose three important tasks. Complete them one by one and avoid letting less important activities take over your time.

  • 📝 Write down your top three tasks.
  • 🎯 Start with the most important one.
  • ⏰ Give each task a realistic time.
  • 📵 Reduce unnecessary distractions.
  • ✅ Review your progress at the end of the day.

KEY LESSON

Remember This

Being busy does not always mean being productive. Put your most important responsibilities first, manage your time thoughtfully and make room for rest and other activities afterwards.
